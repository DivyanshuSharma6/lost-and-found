Guidelines on recovering your belongings inside Manipal University Jaipur

You can hold the item and arrange an anonymous handover in a public area (such as the Central Library desk or Student Activity Area), or hand it directly to the Central Security Control Room near Gate 1.

The platform applies TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency) text modeling and Pillow Computer Vision color extraction. When a match score hits 70% or higher, the system alerts the owner automatically.

For critical items like room keys, wallets, or government IDs, students can activate High-Alert Priority (₹49) to pin the listing to the top of the campus feed and flag it in the security dashboard.
About MUJ Campus Radar

MUJ Campus Radar is an intelligent asset recovery ecosystem engineered to replace chaotic social media messages and physical notice boards across Manipal University Jaipur.

System Highlights
  • NLP Matching: Scikit-learn cosine similarity pairs lost reports with found reports.
  • Computer Vision: Auto-extracts primary color tags from item photos.
  • Contactless Privacy: Share live Leaflet GPS coordinates without disclosing personal phone numbers.
  • Audited Handovers: Download official PDF clearance slips for security checkpoints.
Developed as a Major Academic Capstone Project • Manipal University Jaipur.

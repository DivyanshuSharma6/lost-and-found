About MUJ Campus Radar

MUJ Campus Radar is an academic initiative designed to modernize asset recovery at Manipal University Jaipur. Traditional manual notice boards and informal WhatsApp threads often lead to lost reports, communication delays, and security risks.

Our centralized platform brings together automated machine-learning matching, confidential ownership claim workflows, and real-time physical handover tools to ensure misplaced belongings are returned swiftly to their verified owners.

AI Similarity Engine

Computes lexical and color matches between lost and found items automatically.

Clearance Certificates

Authorized PDF handover clearance for presentation at the security desk.

Campus Recovery Workflow
  • 1
    Report with Photos & Zone
    Log lost or found belongings with campus location tags and images.
  • 2
    Automated Match Evaluation
    The system scans candidate listings and alerts users to high-confidence matches.
  • 3
    Blind Proof Verification
    Claimants submit verification proofs evaluated by campus security administrators.
  • 4
    Live GPS & Safe Handover
    Coordinate handover via in-app chat and interactive Leaflet map tracking.
